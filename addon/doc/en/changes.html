Changes

Version 4.1.0

  • Adds accessible MP3 output bitrate choices: 128, 192, 256, and 320 kbps.
  • Adds MP4 maximum resolution choices: 360p, 480p, 720p, and 1080p.
  • Uses the selected quality for all playlist items and retries.
  • Plays short sounds at the actual start and successful completion of a download.

Version 4.0.0

  • Continues version numbering after the previously published 3.3.3 release.
  • Changes the default shortcut to NVDA+Alt+Y to avoid the comments and notes command conflict.
  • Clarifies the accessible, NVDA-integrated workflow.

Version 3.3.3

  • Downloads individual videos and playlists as MP3 or MP4.
  • Provides an accessible interface and announces progress through NVDA.
  • Uses anonymous downloads and handles unavailable items without repeating the full playlist.
  • Includes Portuguese and English help and compatibility through NVDA 2026.1.
