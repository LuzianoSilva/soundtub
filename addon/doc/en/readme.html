SoundTub 3.3.3

SoundTub provides an accessible NVDA interface for downloading MP3 audio and MP4 video.

Usage

  1. Press NVDA+Alt+C.
  2. Paste the content URL.
  3. Select MP3 or MP4 and a destination folder.
  4. To download every item, check “Download complete playlist”. It is unchecked by default.
  5. Activate Download.

Playlists are saved in their own folder, with files numbered in their original order. Check the item count and available disk space before starting a large playlist.

If an item fails, SoundTub retries only the missing items. If a video is private, removed, or rejected by the service, completed files are preserved and the completed count is reported.

NVDA announces URL analysis, anonymous authorization when needed, the start, and the progress of the download. The add-on does not read browser cookies and does not require an open browser.

Only download content you are authorized to save. Private, restricted, or protected content may not work without authentication.
